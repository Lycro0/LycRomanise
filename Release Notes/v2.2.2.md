## LycRomanise v2.2.2

### Fixed
- Japanese lyrics are fully romanized again. Since v2.0, kanji could be left as they were ("夢o見ta" instead of "yume o mita"), because Windows' Japanese reader only works on one kind of thread and the app was asking it from another. The reader now always runs on a thread it accepts, and the built-in tests check Japanese the way the app really runs it.
- Readings written into a lyric after kanji, like "天使（てんし）" in some KuGou lyrics, are removed, so they no longer show twice.

Download `LycRomanise_Setup.exe` (the installer, recommended) or `LycRomanise.exe` (portable). If you're on v2.x, the app will offer this update itself.
