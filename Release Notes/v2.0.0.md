## LycRomanise v2.0.0: the rewrite

LycRomanise has been rewritten from scratch. It's now a native Windows app in **C# / .NET 10** instead of Python: one self-contained exe that starts faster, draws on the GPU and uses no CPU while your music is paused. Your v1 colours, strip width, timing offset and web view settings are carried over on first start.

### New
- **Word-by-word karaoke.** The sung colour now sweeps each word as it is sung, with a soft edge, wherever the lyrics are word-timed. Lines slide and fade into place, and three dots count down through instrumental breaks.
- **Full lyrics window, Apple Music style.** A blurred, slowly drifting cover behind every line of the song. The current line fills word by word while the rest are dimmed, and the page glides along with a staggered wave. Scroll freely, click a line to jump there, click the progress bar to seek. Open it with Ctrl + Alt + L, by double-clicking the strip, from the tray, or from the new **Full lyrics** bar in Settings.
- **Ten lyric sources**, asked in order: Spicy Lyrics, BiniLyrics, your own imported files, Musixmatch, Unison, NetEase, LRCLIB, QQ Music, KuGou and Genius. Reorder them or switch any off. Word sync wins over line sync.
- **Duets, backing vocals and translations**, where the source has them.
- **Better romanization.** Japanese kanji are read in context by Windows' own Japanese dictionary, Chinese gets tone-marked pinyin, Korean follows the pronunciation rules, and when a source ships its own romanization (Apple Music), that is used, timed per word.
- **Choose lyrics.** Asks every source at once, shows a preview of each, and remembers your pick for that song.
- **Import your own lyrics** (.lrc, enhanced word-timed .lrc, .ttml, .txt) per song or by folder.
- **Optional Spotify login.** Looks up the next song's lyrics before it starts and uses the exact track ID.
- **Global shortcuts.** Ctrl + Alt + L / K / H / ← / → / Space.
- **All-new Settings** in the Windows 11 style, with light and dark mode, colour presets, fonts, glow and background.
- **Automatic updates.** Checks GitHub and installs new versions for you, verifying each download's checksum.

### Kept from v1
The web view for phones and OBS (now with a transparent `?obs=1` link), `LycRomanise.txt` for OBS text sources, per-song timing, lock/click-through, hiding the strip, and start with Windows.

### Upgrading
The installer upgrades v1 in place. Settings now live in `%APPDATA%\LycRomanise Modern`.

**Downloads:** `LycRomanise_Setup.exe` (installer, recommended) or `LycRomanise.exe` (portable).
