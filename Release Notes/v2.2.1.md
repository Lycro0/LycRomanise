## LycRomanise v2.2.1

This release fixes what a new set of built-in tests found. About 28,000 checks now cover romanization, every lyric file format, damaged settings files, drawing every look, lookups for 29 real songs in five languages, and the whole app running with simulated playback.

### Full lyrics window
- Words that lift or glow as they're sung are no longer sliced off at the top or bottom. Each word is now drawn from its own outline.
- A settings panel opens from the gear button or with S. It has the motion style, a blurred-cover or plain black background, romanized, original or both, translations, backing vocals, text size, timing for the current song, keep on top, the player panel, and a link to all settings.
- The desktop strip hides while the window is open and comes back when you close or minimize it. You can turn this off in the panel or in Settings.
- The buttons fade out while you watch and come back when you move the mouse. This can be turned off too.
- The window opens on the current line instead of scrolling down to it from the top.

### Romanization
- Korean lyrics timed syllable by syllable now get the right sound changes: 말해줄래 is "malhaejullae" (it was "malhaejulrae"), 멀리 is "meolli" and 멀어 is "meoreo". Korean also always uses LycRomanise's own romanization, because Apple's splits every syllable ("i yu" instead of "iyu").
- In Japanese, a source's romaji that left kanji in place ("悲shimanai明日o願ukedo") or ran a whole line together is replaced with a proper reading. っ stays with its word ("natte ita"), and こんにちは reads "konnichiwa".
- Traditional Chinese characters get their everyday readings: 也許 is yě xǔ, 幾 is jǐ, 晃 is huàng, and 們 and 說 are fixed too.

### Display
- The desktop strip no longer shows where the lyrics came from, for any source. The full lyrics window and Settings still show it.
- Very long lines shrink to fit the strip. Before, the last words were dropped.
- Emoji in lyrics show up. They used to leave gaps.
- Long lines without spaces wrap on the phone web view and the OBS page instead of running off the edge.
- The full lyrics window does about a third less drawing work, and romanized lines are remembered, so a song's lyrics are ready up to 20 times sooner.
- The Minimal layout has room for two rows of romanization, so a long line no longer overflows the strip.
- The app icon is now just "Ly", and the Now playing card in Settings is black and red.

### Lyrics and files
- Skipping to the next song and quickly back no longer leaves the wrong lyrics. Spotify can report a song's title a moment before its length, and the lookup was using the previous song's length. A length that arrives late now starts a fresh lookup, and when you're logged in to Spotify its own length is used.
- LRC files saved with a byte order mark (Notepad often adds one) no longer lose their first line.
- Short artist names have to match exactly, so "V" no longer matches every artist with a v in their name.
- Impossible values in a damaged settings file are ignored. Before, they could crash the app or draw text at size 0.
- One bad value in the settings file no longer resets the rest. The unreadable file is kept as a backup.

Download `LycRomanise_Setup.exe` (the installer, recommended) or `LycRomanise.exe` (portable). If you're on v2.x, the app will offer this update itself.
