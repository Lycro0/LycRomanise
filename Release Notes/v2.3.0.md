## LycRomanise v2.3.0

### Lighter again
- The desktop strip redraws at most 30 times a second instead of 60, cutting its CPU use while a song plays by about a quarter.
- The full lyrics window redraws at most 30 times a second, and stops redrawing once a line is fully sung until the next one starts.
- The lyrics window creates less garbage per frame.

### Settings
- Removed hint lines that only repeated the option's name.

Everything from v2.2.5 (lower CPU and memory, timing, colour-saving, sweep and glow fixes) is included.

Download `LycRomanise_Setup.exe` (the installer, recommended) or `LycRomanise.exe` (portable). If you're on v2.x, the app will offer this update itself.
