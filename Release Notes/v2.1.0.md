## LycRomanise v2.1.0: looks, and a new web view

### New
- **Nine looks for the strip**, picked from a gallery in Settings → Appearance:
  - **Classic**: the original LycRomanise layout is back. The line being sung sits at the top with the next line straight under it, and the next line rises and grows into place. It's the default.
  - **Modern**, **Apple Music** (words light up, lift and glow on frosted glass), **Spotify**, **Neon**, **Karaoke**, **Minimal**, **Sunset** and **Mint**.
- **Strip layouts** (Classic, Modern, Minimal), plus **word lift** and **glow on sung words**, can be mixed with any colours.
- **Motion styles for the full lyrics window**: Apple Music, Glow, Float, Calm and Snappy.
- **The web view now looks like the full lyrics window**: a blurred, drifting cover behind every line, word-by-word fill and smooth scrolling, on phones, tablets and any browser.
- **A separate transparent page for OBS** at `/obs` (copy it from Settings → Web view & OBS). It follows your strip's look, colours and outline. The old `?obs=1` link still works.

### Fixed
- The full lyrics window could open larger than the screen.
- Bright album covers washed out the full lyrics window's background.
- Translucent colours showed the wrong colour on the web pages.

**Downloads:** `LycRomanise_Setup.exe` (installer, recommended) or `LycRomanise.exe` (portable). If you're on v2.0.x, the app offers this update by itself.
