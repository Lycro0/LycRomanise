## LycRomanise v2.2.5

### Much lighter on your PC
- The desktop strip now uses about 80% less CPU while a song plays. It only redraws what actually changes, instead of redrawing everything on every screen refresh.
- Memory use is lower and steadier.
- The full lyrics window is lighter in full screen, and resizing or maximizing it no longer stutters.
- The Settings window opens pages instantly. Pages are kept once built, and the font list and colour swatches load only when you open them.

### Lyrics timing
- Lyrics no longer race ahead after you resume a paused song.
- Small corrections from the player are smoothed in, so lines no longer jump back or rush forward.
- Skipping songs quickly no longer looks up lyrics for the wrong song. Spotify sends the next song's length before its title, and the app now waits for both to match.
- Pausing, playing and song changes are picked up straight away.
- Lyrics whose last word ran into the next line are trimmed, so lines no longer speed through.
- Lines from every lyrics source are now in the right order.

### Visual fixes
- The colour sweep now always reaches the end of the last word, instead of sometimes stopping partway through the last letter.
- In the full lyrics window, the glow is no longer cut off around words, and words that wrap onto two lines are no longer drawn twice.
- The web view and OBS page stop on pause and follow skips at once, instead of running on for up to a second.

### Settings
- Colour changes are saved as soon as you type a full colour, and are no longer lost when you close Settings straight away.
- Text boxes (Spotify client ID, web port, font) are saved even if you close Settings while typing.
- Changes made in the lyrics window are no longer undone by the Settings window.
- Clicking a look no longer throws away your own colours without warning.
- Settings are saved when Windows shuts down or you sign out.

### Romanization
- Korean: fixed readings such as 싫어 (sireo) and 밝히다 (balkida), and compound jamo are romanized.
- Japanese: long lines with emoji are romanized, 々 is read properly, and rarer katakana are covered.
- A single kana no longer makes a Chinese or Korean song read as Japanese.

### Other fixes
- http://localhost:8765 now answers at once. It used to wait about a second per request, which made the OBS page lag.
- A failed or partial lyrics lookup is no longer remembered as "no lyrics" for a day.
- A Spotify server error no longer logs you out.
- Many smaller fixes to lyrics sources, the updater, the tray and hotkeys.

Download `LycRomanise_Setup.exe` (the installer, recommended) or `LycRomanise.exe` (portable). If you're on v2.x, the app will offer this update itself.
